Our Mission

Eliminating Fraud from Property Transactions

In India, over 60% of property litigation happens because buyers skip 30-year archival title verification or buy land with mismatched revenue mutation records.

Title Square inspects Sub-Registrar records, High Court litigation portals, and Jamabandi/Patta files to provide clear advocate-signed opinions.
