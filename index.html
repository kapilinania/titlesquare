Critical Legal Risk • Supreme Court Scrutiny

Why GPA Sales Cannot Legally Transfer Ownership

Under the landmark Supreme Court ruling in Suraj Lamp & Industries vs State of Haryana, a General Power of Attorney (GPA) or Agreement to Sell cannot confer legal title. Sellers who sell via GPA may have revoked the power, or their heirs can legally challenge your possession years later.

How Title Square Protects You:
  • Verify whether the original principal is alive and whether GPA is registered and unrevoked.
  • Draft a mandatory direct registered conveyance deed with all legal heirs as confirming parties.
High Litigation Risk • Partition Claims

Buying Inherited Property Without Official Mutation

Even if a seller holds original parent deeds, without registered partition and updated revenue mutation (Jamabandi/Khatauni), other undisclosed legal heirs (daughters, siblings, coparceners) have an automatic statutory right to file partition suits and freeze your property.

How Title Square Protects You:
  • Audit legal heir certificates and survivorship records from revenue circles.
  • Verify whether Dharitree / Circle Jamabandi reflects exclusive individual mutation.
Medium-High Risk • Undisclosed Builder Loans

Buying Under-Construction Flats Without Title Audit

Builders frequently mortgage the entire project land to banks or NBFCs for construction finance. If you buy without a formal Bank NOC (No Objection Certificate) and Title Clearance, the lending bank holds a primary mortgage lien over your flat!

How Title Square Protects You:
  • Scrutinize Development Agreement, Power of Attorney, and Bank Mortgage releases.
  • Cross-examine RERA disclosures, sanctioned building plans, and fire NOCs.
Statutory Transfer Ban Risk

Assam Tribal Belt Restrictions & Delhi Agricultural Ceilings

In Assam, Chapter X of the Assam Land and Revenue Regulation strictly prohibits land transfer to non-notified classes in tribal belts and blocks. In Delhi NCR, Section 33 of Delhi Land Reforms prohibits fragmented agricultural land sales. Transactions violating these laws are automatically void by statute!

How Title Square Protects You:
  • Verify Circle demarcation, revenue category (Myadi vs Eksona Patta), and tribal status.
  • Ensure all mandatory statutory permissions and DC NOCs are cleared before registration.

Frequently Asked Questions

A property title search report is a certified legal opinion prepared by High Court advocates after scrutinizing Sub-Registrar archives for 30 years. It verifies unbroken ownership transfers, confirms the seller's rightful title, and checks that the property is free from court attachments or undisclosed bank mortgages.

Under Indian property law, the principle of Buyer Beware applies. If you purchase a property with a disputed title, fraudulent GPA, or hidden litigation, you risk losing both your money and the property. Title Square gives you complete legal clarity before you commit.

Our advocates examine 30 years of registered Sale Deeds, Mother Deeds, Encumbrance Certificates, Jamabandi / Khatauni revenue mutation records, building sanction plans, and pending civil litigation records at the relevant courts.
